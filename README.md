# Juwel Rana, PhD, MPH, MSS

Dr. Juwel Rana is an environmental epidemiologist who integrates causal inference methods and exposome approaches to investigate how complex environmental, nutritional, and social exposures jointly shape population health and health inequities across the life course. He earned his PhD in Epidemiology from the Department of Epidemiology, Biostatistics and Occupational Health, McGill University, Canada. He is the Founder and serves as the Director of Research and Innovation at the South Asian Institute for Social Transformation (SAIST). His path began during undergraduate studies at the University of Dhaka, where he laid the groundwork for what became SAIST, initially taking shape as YRIDB and SAYRID on 15 July 2010, driven by a dedicated group of young researchers. Juwel leads its flagship study, the Bangladesh Longitudinal Child-Adolescent Development, Education, and Environment Study (BLADES) cohort (2021), involving children, adolescents, and their family members from ~2,500 households in Dhaka and Dinajpur districts.
